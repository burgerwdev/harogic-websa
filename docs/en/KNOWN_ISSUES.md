# Known Limitations

## Device and SDK

- **Exclusive ownership:** `libhtraapi` requires exclusive access to the device. `Device_Open` returns `-1` while SAStudio4 holds it. The proprietary SDK can block or crash; unplug/replug recovery and shutdown must be qualified on the deployed hardware build.
- **Capture recovery:** a sustained SDK stall ends capture with its original cause. Recovery requires a fresh capture epoch; old timestamps cannot continue. Queue backlog waits under bounded backpressure until explicit failure without reopening the device. A stuck vendor owner remains parked. SDK finish and reopen are refused during its vendor call. Capture failure uses global error handling.
- **External reference:** supply a 10 MHz reference and set `ExternalSystemClockFrequency` accordingly. `Ext` falls back to the internal reference on unlock; `ExtForce` forces external reference use. Do not set `SystemClockSource=External`: it can hang the device.
- **GNSS calibration:** some firmware leaves `RefClkFreqOffset` at zero; use the computed offset after calibration. GNSS/1PPS calibration and 6/7/9 GHz soak qualification remain outstanding.
- **IF AGC:** `EnableIFAGC=0` is the default, consistent with the vendor profile. Its effect under IF saturation remains unqualified.
- **Factory calibration:** calibration is stored in firmware. Do not load an external frequency-response file with `Devcie_SetFreqResponseCompensation`.
- **Internal spurs:** integer-GHz spurs, including 1.000 and 2.000 GHz, affect SWP/RTA/SDR. Their apparent level can rise with input attenuation; narrow RBW can expose them in SWP. Treat them as device artefacts when interpreting a trace.
- **Trace grid:** the device-native grid determines the available points; a requested point count is not a guarantee of display resolution. The firmware ignores `SWP_Profile_TypeDef.TraceBinSize_Hz`, so fixed bin spacing is unsupported. `SET_TRACEGRID.points_strategy` accepts `speed` and `accuracy`.

## Measurements and triggers

- **Normalization:** the external sweep source must cover the measurement bandwidth, with dwell time at least as long as the analyzer hop time. A CW source only normalizes its own frequency effectively; the baseline is clamped to zero.
- **Phase noise:** offsets at the 100 Hz and 10 MHz boundaries are inaccurate. Minimum input level is -50 dBm.
- **Channel measurements:** channel power, OBW and ACPR use the displayed trace and are limited by its bin spacing. A narrow CW signal can occupy only one or two bins even with a much narrower RBW. For finer OBW resolution, reduce span or increase the available point count.
- **RTA level trigger:** triggering requires a threshold crossing. A steady signal already above the threshold when armed can wait indefinitely. `bus` is the supported RTA trigger source. POI describes the shortest burst the configuration is guaranteed to capture. Vendor timeout is also a communication timeout, so waiting can remain unknown; finite capture completion requires device/frame evidence. Fixture input does not simulate hardware hits.
- **Swept-mode level trigger:** SWP has no device level-trigger field. The frontend detects crossings between consecutive sweeps, making one sweep the minimum time unit. The display remains live while armed and freezes on a hit. RTA timing controls and POI are unavailable in swept mode.

## SDR

- **Zero-IF artefact:** IQ DC and LO leakage sit at the requested capture centre. A signal exactly at that frequency overlaps the artefact.
- **Upper-edge gap:** the capture grid can end before the upper edge of the display window. Uncovered bins remain gaps; RTA/SDR do not interpolate them with swept-mode GapFill.
- **Vendor ADM:** SINAD/SNR/THD require a dominant modulation tone. They are not meaningful quality indicators for ordinary broadcast audio; raw values remain available in STATUS `sdr.adm`.
- **Squelch level:** the pre-AGC tuned-bin peak is not integrated channel power. Modulation spreads power across bins, so set the squelch threshold for the received signal and mode.

## Receiver and browser boundaries

- **Receiver capacity:** sixteen independent definitions share one capture. Disabled definitions retain a definition slot; deleting frees it. Every complete passband and transition must fit the shared capture. Ordinary narrowband operation covers the original capture choices, including a 50 MHz usable window at 62.5 MSps with 16 taps per phase. Wideband modes, searches, neural reducers and external decoders have no predicted CPU/output or algorithm-count admission gate. Actual throughput depends on this host and input; bounded per-receiver queues fail only the affected processing branch, retain independent IQ and allow explicit retry. Native crashes, shared source failure and CPU saturation remain larger-scope boundaries. The workbench supports selection, local mixing and sixteen service-wide recording tasks. Internal PFB bin counts and the long-term capacity target do not describe current receiver capacity. Sixteen-channel sustained RF validation is pending. The earlier eight-channel per-receiver isolation build passed thirty minutes of ordinary RF, eleven minutes of mixed RF, and more than ten minutes of eight-task recording at 50 MHz / 62.5 MSps / 16 taps per phase on the tested host. Known tones, FT8 results, network PCM/IQ continuity, independent archive reads and real RF browser operations were checked. This is not a claim of physical loudspeaker listening or arbitrary heavy-model capacity.
- **Host load at full bandwidth:** earlier runs overflowed the USB handoff; same-input layered tests also reproduced this on the previous binary under finite owned CPU pulses. The PFB pool now admits one job per existing worker instead of blocking at three jobs for four workers. The sustained RF checks above passed without changing unrelated processes, USB queue capacity, capture bandwidth or filter quality. Temporary queue buildup still occurs; system-wide saturation, vendor stalls and shared capture failure remain explicit limits. These measurements do not guarantee uninterrupted reception under arbitrary host load.
- **Raw DSP input:** direct `Pipeline` and `AnalogDemod` trait callers must supply finite input. Production `Receiver` and offline entry points check for nonfinite input; the raw DSP API does not.
- **RDS:** decoded text quality remains constrained; stereo indication reports pilot presence, not different left/right programme content.
- **AM:** modulation depth is single-tone-equivalent RMS envelope depth, not arbitrary programme peak depth. Overmodulation folds the absolute envelope and can under-report signed depth; values are not capped at 100%. Carrier fading invalidates offset/depth and clears lock. The recovery estimator is limited to `min(IF bandwidth * 0.2, receiver IQ rate * 0.1, 3000 Hz)` and excludes lock near that boundary; this is a pull-range limit, not a guarantee of acquisition. Sync enabled does not itself mean carrier locked; these metrics do not establish real-RF reception quality.
- **FT8:** decoding supports standard message types 1/2 within the supported search window, not unrestricted message types or searches.
- **SSTV:** frequency offset is estimated from each valid VIS header and is not continuously tracked within a picture. The internal passband is fixed at 300–2700 Hz. Post-image FSK callsign decoding is unavailable.
- **Radiofax:** automatic IOC 288/576 and 60/90/120/240 LPM are supported; 180 LPM is manual. Each storage segment holds at most 2400 rows. A real midstream recording verified unlocked IOC576/120 LPM fallback. Pluto-to-SAN-90 RF at 435 MHz also produced complete 1810×64 (IOC576/120 LPM) and 905×256 (IOC288/240 LPM) pages, with phasing clock recovery and confirmed APT stop. Both pages had no missing rows. These bench results do not establish other rate/modulation combinations, long-page drift, frequency-offset or fading interoperability.
- **Radiofax retention:** history is limited to the current browser session and 20 MiB of allocated Gray8 per channel. Current and viewed pages are protected; when they fill the budget, further retention stops with a visible status. Save needed pages, then delete pages to free space; the stopped transmission is not backfilled. The backend snapshot is also bounded and can omit older storage segments of a long page. Whole-page PNG export is limited to 32767 pixels per dimension and 16777216 pixels; larger or incomplete retained pages must be exported by segment. Receipt dates identify the browser's first image row, not the station's transmission or chart validity time.
- **DRM:** short or looped codec playback can underrun; such clips do not establish sustained playback continuity. Codec audio bypasses the receiver AGC, noise reduction, squelch and de-emphasis controls, which are disabled in the UI; volume and audio enable remain usable. NR preferences remain stored with effective NR Off. Full signalling reconfiguration and next-multiplex timing are not fully applied.
- **External decoders:** installed capability is not a signal lock or an RF interoperability claim. The optional multimon-ng and rtl_433 processes require their exact input formats; supported sensor protocols depend on the installed rtl_433 build. OnAir Web integration covers AIS/APRS/FT4/WSPR/Meteor/ADS-B, with RTTY supplied by a separately built OnAir companion; this is not the complete desktop catalog, digital voice codecs or TV media output. Wide descriptor rates use the direct raw IQ branch subject to capture bandwidth and transition geometry; actual overload is reported by the affected branch. Integer decimation publishes its actual IQ rate; the receiver adapter supplies exact 4 MSps GPS or 2 MSps ADS-B input. The route does not itself establish hardware reception or provide DAB/TV decoders.
- **OnAir input and timing:** IQ is fixed-scale 8-bit over a private loopback source. AIS requires the 162 MHz centre of its fixed channel pair; other upstream band restrictions remain enforced. FT4/WSPR require UTC-aligned live input; unaligned recording/looping-fixture replay is rejected. EOF has a bounded drain but no upstream completion acknowledgement. Meteor currently emits partial image strips without an end-of-picture indication.
- **External verification scope:** recorded Acurite IQ and recorded POCSAG discriminator audio exercised real rtl_433/multimon decoding, including receiver rate adaptation. Synthetic AIS/APRS/Meteor IQ exercised their structured outputs; UTC-aligned clean synthetic input produced FT4/WSPR messages through the real Receiver and OnAir bridge. These checks establish those paths, not RF sensitivity, fading behavior or individual qualification of every multimon module.
- **Translated RF bench verification:** Pluto-to-SAN-90 reception at a 435 MHz bench centre and Pluto gain −30 dB decoded DTMF `123A#`, APRS `N0CALL-1` with `WEBSA APRS TEST`, AIS MMSI `367000001` with the expected position, FT4 `CQ K1ABC FN42`, and WSPR `K1ABC FN42 30`. Each transmission stopped after its first matching result; no receiver error was reported. These generated signals establish the tested hardware paths, not passive on-air reception or calibrated sensitivity.
- **ADS-B and RTTY offline verification:** 40 million known ADS-B IQ samples at 4 MSps passed through the production Receiver and 2 MSps OnAir input, decoding all 270 transmitted frames, ICAO `40621D`, callsign `EZY123`, altitude 38000 ft and independently checked CPR coordinates. Normal and reverse 45.45-baud/170-Hz RTTY fixtures decoded the full known text, digits and punctuation with zero framing errors through the production Receiver. These are synthetic IQ checks.
- **RTTY RF verification:** normal and reverse modes both decoded `CQ CQ DE WEBSA TEST 0123456789 1/2 -.:? THE QUICK BROWN FOX` over the 435 MHz Pluto/SAN-90 bench at gain −30 dB, using the existing USB WAV transmitter. Each completed within 31 seconds and stopped on the matching text without receiver errors.
- **GPS L1 C/A:** the optional native bridge runs GNSS-SDR against SDR IQ at exactly 4 MSps and normally 1575.420 MHz; it does not consume the SDK's built-in GNSS receiver status. Explicit `WEBSA_GPS_BENCH_HZ` enables reception at one exact translated 400–450 MHz bench frequency, marked separately in results; it does not configure a transmitter. Satellite tracking/navigation and PVT validity are separate, stale fixes expire, and startup remains explicitly no-fix. Installed capability does not establish sky reception or an offline-recording pass. GNSS-SDR's FIFO never acknowledges end-of-input, so shutdown uses a bounded result drain and reports unknown upstream completion. Generated translated reception does not establish passive GPS reception; no GPS L1-band transmission is provided.
- **GPS recorded verification:** the complete CTTC 2013-04-04 recording passed through the production Receiver at 4 MSps: 400 million complex samples forwarded, zero malformed monitor packets, five tracked PRNs, 67 decoded navigation subframes and 232 valid PVT snapshots. The final solution was 41.2748489°, 1.98766735°, height 78.13 m, GPS week 1734. This establishes real-recording decoding; passive sky reception and translated bench RF require separate evidence.
- **GPS generated-signal verification:** a 60-second sample-counted scenario at 2.6 MSps passed through the production Receiver's 4 MSps adapter and GNSS-SDR, producing 77 valid PVT snapshots with seven satellites, 77 navigation subframes across 11 PRNs, and zero malformed monitor packets. The final horizontal error against 25.2° N, 55.36° E was 0.54 m. This was an offline synthetic input; it does not establish RF reception.
- **GPS translated RF verification:** a cold-start 3.90625 MSps SAN-90 capture at 435 MHz received the Pluto-generated scenario at gain −30 dB and produced a valid four-satellite fix within 63.41 seconds. Horizontal error was 3.65 m against the configured scenario position; no receiver error occurred. TX stopped on the first accepted fix and the Pluto reference clock was restored. This validates the translated bench path, not passive sky reception.
- **Generic Decode retention:** provider results are browser-session local, capped at 200 results / 8 MiB per channel including decoded strip memory, with 512 strips per picture. Evictions are visible; late subscribers do not receive a WebSA historical generic-result snapshot. Generic images currently have no PNG export action. Native SSTV/Radiofax retention and export retain their separate contracts. The popup shares the opener's session and closes with it.
- **Browser rendering:** a 20 fps acquisition target does not guarantee 20 fps for every display layer. Existing observations with spectrum, density, waterfall and navigation enabled are around 15 fps; this is not a fresh hardware or browser measurement.

For device constraints and probe context, see [SDR probe findings](../../tools/sdr_probe/FINDINGS.md). Hardware observations there are configuration-specific; this document does not establish qualification of a deployed build.
